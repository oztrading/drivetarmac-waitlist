FAQ

Good questions.

When does it launch?

We're finishing samples now. The founders list hears first, before anyone else, with the exact date and your $24 founder link.

What do founders get?

The first 500 people on the list get the Starter Kit (clip + one 10 mL scent) for $24 instead of $29.95, plus a free bottle of Garage, our limited scent, sent when it drops in January.

How do refills work?

Two 10 mL bottles every two months for $24.95. Mix any two scents. Skip, swap, or cancel anytime. Refills are optional.

Will it fit my car?

The clip is designed for most horizontal-slat vents, with a magnetic visor mount as a backup. We're testing it across cars before launch.

How long does a bottle last?

It depends on heat, airflow, and how many drops you use. We're testing in real cars and will publish what we find, not a guess.

Where do you ship?

US only at launch, by ground.
